Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Mostly so I don't lose my work. Git saves snapshots of everything so if I mess something up I can just go back to an older version instead of trying to remember what I changed. It also makes group projects way less painful since everyone can work on their own branch and combine stuff later instead of sending files back and forth.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Sports. Like in basketball, teams use tracking data on shot locations and player movement to figure out which shots are actually worth taking, which is a big part of why everyone shoots so many threes now.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging takes two branches and combines them with a new merge commit, so you can still see in the log that the branches split off and came back together. Rebasing moves your commits and puts them on top of the other branch like they were made there all along, so you get one straight line of commits and no merge commit. Merging keeps the real history, rebasing gives you a cleaner one.
